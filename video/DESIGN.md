# DebugMate V0.1 Video Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Technical course explainer with a calm institutional voice: dark navy canvas, warm off-white text, restrained orange emphasis, and cool teal data accents. The visual language should feel like a dependable engineering notebook rather than a commercial launch. Use large Chinese typography, simple evidence cards, real interface screenshots, and directional motion that communicates workflow and causality.

## Colors

- Background: `#122B46` deep navy
- Foreground: `#F1F5F7` warm off-white
- Accent: `#EB713E` safety orange
- Data accent: `#2BA6B5` teal
- Muted text: `#AFC3D1` blue gray

## Typography

- Chinese headline/body: `Microsoft YaHei`, weights 900 and 350
- Technical IDs/data: `Cascadia Mono`, weight 400 with tabular numbers

## Motion

- Medium-energy technical explainer.
- Primary transition: horizontal push slide, 0.4 seconds, `power2.inOut`.
- Accent transition for topic changes: orange wipe, 0.45 seconds.
- Entrances combine small position changes, opacity, and scale with varied eases.
- Persistent slow-moving line/grid decoratives provide depth without neon effects.

## What NOT to Do

- No purple/blue commercial gradients or neon cyberpunk glow.
- No generic equal-weight card grids when sequence or causality is clearer.
- No tiny body text, dense paragraph walls, or raw terminal screenshots without framing.
- No fake cloud-run claims, fake metrics, or generated screenshots presented as evidence.
- No jump cuts; every scene change uses the defined transition language.
